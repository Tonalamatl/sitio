In short: we keep your email (or, if you sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or TikTok, what that service gives us to recognize you), your name, your gender, the birth date you enter and those of your people, your subscription status and, if you invite someone or were invited, how those invitations are going. What we use it for

Everything we handle is there to make the app work:

  • Your email: to create your account, verify it with a code, let you sign in and help you recover it. We only email you when you ask for it: when you create your account and when you change your password.
  • What your sign-in service gives us: to let you in without another password. If that service has verified your email and you already have an account with that same email, we link them, so you don’t end up with two.
  • Your first and last name: so the app can call you by your name.
  • Your gender: it doesn’t change your reading. We keep it so that, if you ever choose to hear about courses and ceremonies, you get the ones meant for you —some ceremonies are only for women and others only for men—. If you choose “prefer not to say”, nothing changes.
  • Where you were born: it’s part of your birth information, and it tells us where the people using the app come from. We don’t use it to locate you.
  • The dates, yours and your people’s: to work out their Tonals, which is what you asked for when you saved them, and so they aren’t lost if you change phones. If you enter a birth time, it’s used to place it against the start of the day in this count. It’s also kept for your future readings.
  • Your plan: to know what you have unlocked, and to restore your subscription if you change phones.
  • The reading count: to set usage limits so no one can extract the full body of the teaching.
  • Your phone’s fingerprint: so the 13-day trial is one per device, and so a subscription bought without an account belongs to that phone and isn’t lost if you reinstall the app.
  • Invitations: to count when someone you invited has used the app on three days, to give you your free month once there are three, and to give whoever arrives with your code their 20-day trial. Whoever arrives with your code sees your name: “Ana invited you”. We don’t tell you who arrived with your code, only how many.
  • The notification ID: to let you know about something that happened in your account, like earning your free month, and, on Android, to let you know when each day starts in your count. We don’t use it for advertising.
  • How you use the app: to see which parts get used, where people get stuck and what breaks, so we can fix it and improve the app. We don’t use it for advertising or to build a profile of you.

We don’t use your data for anything else today. We don’t send you ads or promotions. If we ever want to use it for something else —for example, to tell you about a workshop—, we’ll ask you first, separately, and you can say no without losing anything.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile (first and last name, gender, place and date of birth), your people, your plan and what your sign-in service gives us: for as long as you have an account.
  • The reading count and the count of codes sent: deleted automatically within 48 hours at most.
  • Your phone’s fingerprint, with its 13-day trial and anything you bought without an account: for as long as the app needs it to know what that phone has unlocked. If you want it deleted, write to us.
  • Your invitations and your free month: for as long as you have an account, or as long as the app needs your phone’s fingerprint if you don’t have one.
  • The notification ID: until Apple or Google tell us it no longer works, or until you delete your account.
  • The notifications that a day is starting (Android): until they’re sent. If one couldn’t be sent, it’s deleted automatically one day after its time.
  • How you use the app: up to one year. After that it’s deleted automatically.
  • Deleting your account deletes your profile, your people, your plan, your count and your invite codes. The only thing kept is the record of the charges the store made, without your name or email, because tax law may require us to keep it.

How we protect it

It’s encrypted in transit and at rest. Your profile only opens with your own session’s signature: there is no screen where anyone can look up someone else’s profile.

Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it or object to its use, and withdraw your consent.

  • You can delete your whole account yourself, from the app, or as explained at tonalamatl.app/borrar-cuenta.
  • For anything else, write to apps@tonalamatl.app from your account’s email and tell us what you need. We’ll reply within 20 business days at most and, if it applies, we’ll carry it out within the following 15 business days.

If you’re not satisfied, you can turn to Mexico’s Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, the personal data protection authority.

Adults only

Tonalamatl is for people 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from minors. If you know of an account that belongs to a minor, write to us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, we’ll publish the new version here with its date and let you know in the app before it applies. If the change adds a new use of your data, we’ll ask you again instead of assuming you agree.
